Shorts Studio Publisher

Shorts Studio Publisher is a personal automation tool used exclusively by its owner to schedule, upload, and manage short-form video content across YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram channels.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This application is for personal use only by the channel owner. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from any third-party users.

API access tokens obtained from Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) are stored locally on the owner's private device solely to publish videos and retrieve channel analytics for the owner's own channels.
